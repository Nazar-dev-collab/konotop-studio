06 — FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost, and what affects the price?

A landing page starts at UAH 8,000, a small business or portfolio site at UAH 15,000, a corporate website at UAH 25,000, and an online store or complex project at UAH 40,000. The final price depends on the number of pages and features (forms, animations, a backend, databases), so we name the exact figure once we understand your task. A redesign usually costs about 60–70% of a new website.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timing depends on the scope and how quickly decisions are made, so we give it after the first conversation, before work starts.

How do I get started, and what should I prepare?

Write a few sentences in the form on the site (it opens Telegram or email with your message ready to send) or message @m_konotop on Telegram directly. Just tell us what your business does, which pages you need and when you would like to launch. If you already have a logo, texts, photos or examples of websites you like, include them; we’ll talk through the rest in our first conversation.

How does payment work?

We discuss and agree on the price, payment stages and payment method before work starts, so you know in advance how much to pay and when.

Can I edit the texts and photos on the website myself?

Yes, if we plan for it at the start. Tell us what you want to update yourself (texts, photos, prices or products), and we will build in an easy-to-use admin panel. This affects the scope of work, so it is included in the price.

Who takes care of hosting and the domain?

We help you choose and connect a domain and hosting, and we publish the website at launch. The domain and hosting are usually registered in your name and paid for separately from development, so the website and its address belong to you.

Do you support the website after launch?

Yes, we stay in touch after launch: if a bug turns up in our work, we fix it. Further changes, new sections or ongoing maintenance are discussed separately.

Do you work with clients outside Kropyvnytskyi?

Yes. The whole project can run online: we talk via Telegram, email or a call, so there is no need to meet in person.
